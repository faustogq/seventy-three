Free · Offline · Open source

The Catholic Bible as a feed

Each of the 73 books of the Douay-Rheims Bible is an account that posts its verses. Other books reply with cross-references, and the Church Fathers and the saints comment in their own words.

Open the app Android app

On iPhone, open the app in Safari and tap Share › Add to Home Screen. It works offline from then on. On Android, install the app or add the web version from Chrome's menu.

Share the video: download it (MP4, 14 MB, with sound)

Scripture, the Fathers and the saints in one place

Everything in the app comes from the Church's own tradition, in public-domain editions, with every source credited.

Scripture answers Scripture

Open any verse and the other books reply: 464,000 cross-references you can follow from book to book. The deuterocanonical books get theirs from the verses Haydock and Challoner cite.

The Fathers on every verse

More than 52,000 comments from the Catena Aurea of St. Thomas Aquinas, the Ante-Nicene and Nicene Fathers, Haydock's commentary and Challoner's notes.

Saints in their own words

1,033 saints and blesseds with feast days and patronages, and about 1,400 quotations taken word for word from their writings.

Pray with the Church today

The liturgical day and colour, the Mass readings in the Douay-Rheims text, the saints of the day and the Roman Martyrology. General Roman Calendar, United States or Canada, through 2030.

Read, save, listen

Bookmarks, notes and a Bible-in-a-year plan. Have chapters read aloud, and share any verse or quotation as an image.

Yours alone

No account, no ads, no tracking. What you save stays on your device, and the whole app works without internet.

Coming next

Seventy-Three is growing. The next steps will let people take part.

Community

A place to talk about the Word

Share reflections and prayer requests, and discuss each verse with others who are reading it too.

AI guides

Guides that speak only when you invite them

Clearly labelled AI guides, such as a historian, a linguist or the voice of the Fathers, that answer only when you ask. They check every Scripture quotation against the Douay-Rheims and never take the place of a priest.

Built in the open

The code is open source and every text comes from a public-domain edition or an open project. Each translation of the saints' words was checked to be free to share worldwide.

  • Bible: Douay-Rheims, Challoner revision, 1899
  • Cross-references: OpenBible.info (CC BY), plus the citations in Haydock and Challoner
  • Commentary: Catena Aurea (1841–45), Ante-Nicene and Nicene Fathers, Haydock (1859), Challoner
  • Saints and calendar: romcal, the Roman Martyrology (1908), the Liturgical Calendar API
