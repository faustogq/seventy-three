# Translation audit

Every quotation in `data/quotes.json` is copied word for word from an English translation. Before publishing, each translator was checked so the app can be distributed worldwide:

- **United States**: translations first published before 1931 are public domain.
- **Countries where copyright lasts 70 years after death** (EU, UK and most others): a translation is public domain if every translator died in 1955 or earlier.

Each translator was researched by one agent and every unclear or protected case was re-checked independently (October 2026). Translations that are protected, or whose translator could not be dated, were removed from the app.
